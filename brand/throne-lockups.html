What people call this kind of app

  • Campaign manager is the most common term in app stores and plugins. It's what D&D Beyond's own campaign pages, Game Master 5th Edition and Campaign Codex all use.
  • Companion app is how D&D Beyond and others describe the character-sheet and dice side.
  • DM tools or GM tools is what people search when they run the game.
  • Character manager and campaign notes cover the player side and your headline feature.

Descriptor candidates: Campaign manager for tabletop RPGs (what's shown above), Campaign notes, characters and dice (describes your three strengths), Your campaign companion (shorter and friendlier). I avoided "D&D" in the descriptor, because it's a Wizards of the Coast trademark and "for D&D" can read as an affiliation. Saying it in a search description is normal, but a logo lockup is more exposed.
